How this page is made

Not drawn, and the one page here that is not. These are the stories The New York Times ran on page 1 of each print section — the front page, then each section's front — read from NYT's Article Search API, which records where every article ran in print.

The API records the section and page but not the date of the paper, so this page takes stories published online the day before: NYT fills in print placement once a paper is laid out, and this morning's paper is made of yesterday's online stories. Within a section the order is alphabetical, because the API does not say where on the page a story ran.

Data provided by The New York Times.
